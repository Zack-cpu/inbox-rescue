Before using the Preview

Terms of use

Effective October 22, 2026

Inbox Rescue is an open-source browser extension maintained by Zack-cpu. By using the extension or this website, you agree to these terms. The source code is provided under its MIT license; that license governs rights to the code.

Preview availability

This is an early Preview. Anyone may try fictional-mail demo mode. Real Gmail access currently requires an account permitted by the project’s Google testing configuration and any applicable Google Workspace administrator policies. General Gmail access, Google verification, and store distribution are not complete. Features and availability may change.

Your account and decisions

Connect only an account you are authorized to manage. Read the Google consent screen and privacy policy before granting access. Verify senders before restoring messages. You are responsible for decisions made using the review queue; correspondence evidence and header text do not prove a message is safe.

Functional limits

The extension does not guarantee detection of important mail, message delivery, future Gmail classification, uninterrupted access, or compatibility with every browser environment. Scan counts and time windows are capped. Access tokens expire, and the Preview uses manual updates.

Undo actions are held only in the current panel and can disappear when results are cleared or rescanned, or the panel reloads. Applied Gmail changes remain. Label operations are not transactional with other clients. A failed network request may leave an update result uncertain; inspect Gmail before retrying.

Acceptable use

Use the project consistently with applicable laws, the rights of others, Google’s relevant terms, and your organization’s policies. Do not use it to access accounts without authorization, collect other people’s mail without permission, or distribute credentials. Report vulnerabilities privately rather than posting live tokens or mailbox contents.
