Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · Browser extension Preview and this website

Inbox Rescue is maintained by Zack-cpu, the maintainer of the open-source repository. Contact: yexunyang7@gmail.com. This policy describes the browser extension and this informational website; it does not describe the optional Python prototype.

What we access and why

Only after you choose Connect Gmail and approve Google access, the extension reads your authorized Gmail address, message and conversation identifiers, label information, From/To/Cc/Subject headers, and available Authentication-Results header text. Recent sent correspondence is used to identify addresses you contacted and conversations you participated in. Metadata responses may include snippets; these are not displayed or deliberately persisted.

Scans currently cover up to 300 sent messages from 90 days and up to 100 Spam/Promotions messages from 7 days, excluding Trash. We use this data to display a review queue, explain correspondence matches, and perform the label changes you explicitly request.

Google permissions and label changes

The extension requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify to read metadata and modify message labels for user-confirmed restore and undo. This scope is broad and technically permits sending mail; Inbox Rescue has no send operation. It does not automatically restore messages, permanently delete mail, load attachments or email images, render email HTML, or scrape the Gmail web page.

Processing and sharing

Gmail requests travel directly between the extension in your browser and Google over HTTPS. We do not operate an application backend that receives your mailbox data. This version has no analytics, advertising integration, remote executable code, or third-party AI service. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or generalized AI/model training.

Google processes authorization and API requests under its own policies. If you voluntarily email support or post a GitHub issue, the information you send is handled by those communication services. Avoid sending real mail contents or credentials; use synthetic examples where possible. Support messages are used to respond to your request, not to analyze your mailbox.

Local storage and retention

Access tokens and the connected account address are kept in browser-session storage, not an application disk-token file or sync storage. Tokens are temporary; Google expiry ends their validity, and the extension removes expired session state when next checked. Browser restart/reload behavior follows browser-session storage semantics. The browser and operating system can retain their own state. This is not an encrypted operating-system credential vault.

Fetched message metadata, dismissals, and undo deltas are held in panel memory. Clearing results, rescanning, reconnecting/disconnecting, or reloading the panel can remove them. Label changes already applied remain in Gmail. Legacy development builds may have stored a public OAuth client ID locally; the configured release bundles that public ID. It is not a user token or client secret.

Disconnecting, deletion, and your choices

You can use the fictional demo without granting mailbox access. Real label changes require your restore confirmation or undo action. Disconnect clears local authorization and requests Google token revocation. If revocation cannot be confirmed, remove Inbox Rescue in your Google Account’s third-party connections. Removing the extension clears its local storage but does not itself guarantee Google revocation or reverse earlier Gmail changes.

To clear fetched panel data, clear results or close/reload the panel. To request deletion of information you voluntarily sent to support, contact the support address above. We will address such requests subject to applicable obligations and the capabilities of the communication service used. Do not email access tokens or passwords.

Google API Limited Use

Inbox Rescue’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only for the user-facing mail review and user-confirmed label-management features described here, not for sale, advertising, or generalized AI/model training.

This website

This website is informational. It does not connect to your Gmail, request your Gmail password, or contain a mailbox data collection form. It includes no analytics or advertising scripts. Its hosting platform necessarily handles technical web requests under that platform’s own policies. Links to GitHub, Google, and email open services governed by their own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when practices materially change. Review the policy before connecting to a new release. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to yexunyang7@gmail.com.

This Preview has not completed Google verification. Describing our practices is not a claim of Google approval.
